My Opinion

This aspect of humanity shouldn’t be outright celebrated nor condemned. If it is used for a country’s good, it can bring huge cultural and economic progress that benefits the world. However, if left unchecked or uncontrolled, that wealth could quickly sweep up too much world control. Plus, countries with poor reputations for good reason could use that to wipe their image, erasing past wrongdoings and twisting reality. The enduring presence of this trait over time suggests that the human impulse to convert resources into forms of power is universal among those who have great wealth. Hence, it is beneficial for societies to recognize this tendency and use it responsibly to advance development that benefits the world as a whole, rather than turning it into wasteful competition or overextension into unregulated world power.
